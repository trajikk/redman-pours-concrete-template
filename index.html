ENGINEERED FOR COLORADO CLIMATE

Why Standard Concrete Fails — And How We Build It Right

Colorado's volatile freeze-thaw cycles cause 80% of contractor concrete to heave, spall, and crack within 3 years. Here is our 5-Layer Structural Standard.

01
🚜

Laser-Leveled Road Base & Compaction

We excavate native expansive clay soils and compact 4–6 inches of Class 6 road base to 95% standard proctor density to prevent settling.

02
⛓️

#4 Grade 60 Rebar Structural Grid

We NEVER use flimsy roll-wire mesh. We tie heavy 1/2" Grade 60 steel rebar on 16" centers elevated on concrete dobies so the steel sits exactly in the center of the pour.

03
🏔️

4,500+ PSI Air-Entrained Mountain Mix

Custom-batched concrete with 6-7% microscopic air voids that give freezing moisture room to expand harmlessly without popping or spalling the surface.

04
📐

Deep Hand-Tooled Control Joints

Joints cut to a precise 1/4 slab depth at calculated intervals to relieve sub-surface hydrostatic stress and guarantee zero random cracking.

05
🛡️

Dual-Coat Deep Penetrating UV Sealer

Commercial breathable solvent-based sealant infused with micro-grit polymer. Protects against UV bleaching, oil stains, and aggressive de-icing salts.
