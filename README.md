# Red Man Pours Concrete — Luxury Architectural & Home Services Website Template

A modern, high-converting website template engineered for high-ticket home services contractors, architectural concrete studios, decorative flatwork specialists, and luxury hardscape builders ($10,000–$50,000+ jobs).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🏛️ Pattern & Texture Catalog**: Inspired by industrial concrete suppliers, featuring interactive selectors for Ashlar Roman Slate, European Cobblestone, Reclaimed Wood Plank, Modern Board-Formed Walls, and Heavy Broomed Flatwork.
- **🎚️ Interactive Before & After Renovation Slider**: Draggable, touch-friendly comparison showing broken 1980s slab vs. multi-tone stamped slate patio.
- **📸 High-Resolution Project Showcase & Lightbox**: Expandable full-screen photography for patios, floating cantilevered steps with LED channels, executive driveways, outdoor kitchens, and garage epoxy showrooms.
- **🧮 3D Project Estimator & Material Calculator**: Dynamic square footage slider (150 to 2,000+ sq ft) with live calculation of cubic yardage, project timeline, PSI mix ratings, and price ranges.
- **🛡️ 5-Layer Colorado Freeze-Thaw Engineering System**: Structural education section detailing road base compaction, #4 rebar grids, 4,500+ PSI mountain mixes, deep control joints, and penetrating solvent sealers.
- **🦅 Indigenous Heritage & Honor Section**: Authentic storytelling highlighting 100% Indigenous ownership, integrity, direct owner job site presence, and master craftsmanship.
- **📱 Mobile-First Conversion Architecture**: Fast-loading static HTML/CSS/JS, sticky mobile bottom action bar (1-tap dispatch & instant quote), and interactive lead form with toast notifications.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Core**: Vanilla HTML5 (Semantic, SEO-optimized, OpenGraph meta)
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS (Custom Design Tokens, Glassmorphism, Dark Obsidian Aesthetic, Responsive Flex/Grid)
- **Interactivity**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6, zero heavy dependencies)
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts (`Cinzel`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Space Grotesk`)
